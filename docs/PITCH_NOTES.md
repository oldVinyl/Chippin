# Pitch notes

People already come together to pay for funerals, weddings, school needs and medical bills. Usually that starts with a MoMo number in a WhatsApp group. Chippin gives those contributions a shared home: a pot, a link, a running total and a donor book. Friends can contribute without an account or an app. Organizers choose when funds go out, and everyone can see that timing before they pay. MTN Collections receives contributions; Disbursements handles payouts and refunds. A permanent ledger, status checks and recovery jobs keep delayed responses from turning into duplicate payments. Families, schools and community groups get a clearer record without changing how they help each other. The prototype uses mock or MTN sandbox payments; live service would operate with MTN or a licensed payment partner.
